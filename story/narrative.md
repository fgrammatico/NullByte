# NULLBYTE: Shut It Down

## Background

HEXCORE is a rogue automated system that has taken over a corporate network and locked everyone out. Nobody knows exactly what it wants. A former developer who goes by GHOST managed to get in, leave evidence trails throughout the facility, and then disappear, but not before sending one message: *follow the breadcrumbs*.

Players work together to breach HEXCORE's security layers, follow GHOST's trail, and gain root access to shut the whole system down.

The host runs NullByte in a local Minecraft Bedrock world. One or more players can join the same session. Discoveries, defenses, gates, and victory are all shared; this is a team effort from start to finish. Leaving, dying, or reconnecting does not reset completed work.

Minecraft chat is the portable terminal. Emails and attachments from GHOST and warnings from ZERO are delivered by the computer add-on near each puzzle. Physical rooms, redstone systems, and terminal commands form one connected scenario.

## Characters

### ZERO

HEXCORE's control AI. Not a person. It monitors every command, treats all intruders as candidates, and gets more aggressive the louder the team gets. Its messages are clipped and clinical. It is not on your side.

### GHOST

A former HEXCORE developer who went rogue. Before vanishing, GHOST left evidence throughout the facility (credentials, logs, bypasses, fragments) to give investigators a fighting chance. The notes are wry, informal, and occasionally smug. GHOST's actual motive is never confirmed.

### SENTINEL

HEXCORE's automated defense system. It reacts to noise with patrols, terminal restrictions, and access revocation. It is not bluffing.

### HR_BOT

HEXCORE's intake AI. It thinks the players are job candidates. GHOST may have had something to do with that.

## The World

### Overworld: eth0

The exposed corporate network contains offices, server infrastructure, security operations, and hardware labs. Players begin here and establish their first foothold.

### Nether: eth1

The internal-services segment is protected by active defenses. Players must preserve access while investigating routes deeper into the network.

### The End: eth2

The isolated System Core is a hostile environment. Players must survive active patrols while solving the final technical gates. Root access is possible only after all required evidence and system conditions are satisfied.

## Shared Session

The world has no private progression. Physical changes and discoveries apply to the whole session. Defensive events may revoke active permission, but completed puzzle discoveries stay recorded.

Individual scoring is tracked separately and does not control progression.

## Tone

NullByte is designed for mixed groups: kids, adults, security nerds, and curious beginners. Clues reward observation and correlation. Wrong answers have consequences, but each challenge provides enough in-world evidence to solve it without guessing. GHOST's notes keep things from getting too serious.
